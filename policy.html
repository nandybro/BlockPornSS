Privacy Policy – BlockPorn

Effective Date: 13 October 2026

Privacy Commitment: BlockPorn is engineered around zero data collection for sensitive content. All AI image and video detection runs completely offline on your device processor. We do not store screenshots, we do not upload screen captures, and no screen data ever leaves your device.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Year of birth

This information is securely stored in Firebase and is used only for user identification, account management, subscription management, customer support, and basic personalization of the app experience. We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising purposes.

2. Accessibility Service Permission

BlockPorn uses Android's Accessibility Service strictly to provide core safety features. It is used to:

  • Detect and block adult or explicit content in real time
  • Detect when restricted apps are opened to apply focus limits
  • Automatically exit restricted apps using system navigation actions
  • Monitor app protection settings to prevent unauthorized removal

BlockPorn may take temporary screenshots to analyze content using on-device AI.

Important Privacy Guarantees:

  • Images are processed strictly locally in your device's temporary memory (RAM)
  • Screenshots are never saved to storage
  • Screen data is never uploaded to any cloud server
  • Screen content is never shared with third parties or our team

All content detection runs entirely offline on your device.

3. Data Storage & Security

Personal account data (email, phone number, year of birth) is securely stored in Firebase. Screen data and AI detection results are processed strictly locally on your device and are never stored on external servers or cloud systems. All local temporary data is automatically purged upon app uninstallation. We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical security measures to safeguard your account information.

4. Children’s Privacy

BlockPorn may be installed by parents or guardians on devices used by children. We do not knowingly collect additional personal data from children beyond account credentials provided by the parent or guardian. Parents are responsible for supervising and administering their child’s device usage.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data. We may share limited information only with secure, industry-standard service providers (such as Firebase for account authentication and subscription handling) or if strictly required by applicable law. Local processing data (including screen analysis) is never shared.

6. Your Control & Rights

You maintain full control over your permissions and personal data at all times:

  • You can grant, deny, or revoke permissions (such as Accessibility) at any time via your device's System Settings.
  • You can request complete deletion of your account and associated credentials at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately and permanently purges all locally stored configuration data.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions, feedback, or data privacy requests concerning this Privacy Policy, please contact our team at:
